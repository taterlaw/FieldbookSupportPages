Fieldbook

Work Orders for Field Crews

Fieldbook is an iPad app for electrical contractors. Your crew fills in work orders on the job and sends them to the office, along with a clean PDF report.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPad · iPadOS 17 or later

How it works

Four steps, from the office to the job site and back.

  1. The office sets up the job

    The office puts a short text file for each job in the Fieldbook folder in iCloud Drive. It's named after the PO number and lists the job name, task, contact, phone and address.

  2. The crew downloads it on site

    Type the PO number into Fieldbook and download the job. Its details are already filled in. No internet? Start a blank work order, or start a new one from a job you downloaded earlier.

  3. Fill in the work

    Add the work done, the materials, crew hours and photos.

  4. Send it to the office

    Tap Submit and pick your name. Fieldbook puts the work order and a PDF report in that PO's folder in iCloud Drive, where the office can open them.

Features

What's in the app.

  • Download by PO number

    Type in the PO number and the job details fill themselves in.

  • Works offline

    Drafts save on the iPad. Submit with no signal and the order is marked "Pending upload". It sends by itself when the iPad is back online.

  • Materials catalog

    Over 1,600 electrical materials in 8 groups: wiring, conduit and fittings, boxes, breakers and fuses, devices and plates, lighting, gear, and hardware. Search by name or part number, or add your own items.

  • Crew hours

    Add people from your saved Crew List. Log one or more work periods for each person, or copy one person's times to everyone.

  • Photos

    Add job photos from the iPad's photo library.

  • PDF report

    A clean, one-page report you can print, share, or email with the Mail app.

  • Gallery

    Every work order on the iPad in one place. Search them, and filter by Draft or Submitted.

  • Directions

    Open the job address in Apple Maps.

  • Built-in guide

    A step-by-step guide, written for people who don't use computers much.

Screenshots

A look at Fieldbook on iPad.

For the office

Fieldbook uses iCloud Drive to pass work between the office and the crew. The office and every crew iPad sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on, so they all share one Fieldbook folder. We recommend a dedicated work Apple Account.

The first time Fieldbook runs, it creates the Fieldbook folder, a Templates folder inside it, and an example template.

Setting up a job

Add one template for each job in iCloud Drive › Fieldbook › Templates. Name it after the PO number, like 2513134.txt. Here's what goes inside:

PO: 2513134
Job Name: Northgate Public Library
Task: Emergency lighting test & repair
Contact: Helen Park
Phone: (503) 555-0142
Address: 801 N Killingsworth St, Portland, OR

On site, the crew types 2513134 into Fieldbook to download the job.

Finding submitted work

Submitted work shows up in a folder named after the PO. Files are named by PO, worker and date, like 2513134-phillip-wright-26-12-03.pdf, alongside a matching .wrko work order file.

If the crew submitted while offline, the files show up once their iPad is back online.

Requirements

  • An iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. Fieldbook is for iPad only.
  • An Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on.
  • Internet to download templates and send work. Everything else works offline.

Privacy at a glance

No accounts. No tracking. No ads.

Work orders stay on the iPad and in your business's own iCloud Drive. The Fieldbook team never sees them.

Support

Need help? Open How to Use Fieldbook, the step-by-step guide built into the app. Or email us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

Do I need internet?

Only to download templates and to send finished work. Everything else works offline, including filling in and saving work orders.

What happens if I submit while offline?

The work order is marked "Pending upload". It sends by itself when the iPad is back online.

Where does the office find submitted work?

In the Fieldbook folder in iCloud Drive, inside a folder named after the PO number. Each one has a PDF report and a matching .wrko work order file.

Does it work on iPhone?

No. Fieldbook is for iPad only, with iPadOS 17 or later.

Who can see our work orders?

Anyone who can use your iPads, or who has access to your Apple Account or its Fieldbook folder. The Fieldbook team can't see them. Fieldbook never sends your data to us.

How do I delete our data?

Delete a work order in the app to remove it from the iPad. You can remove templates and Crew List names in the app too. Files already sent to iCloud Drive stay there until you delete them in the Files app or on iCloud.com. Deleting the app removes its data from the iPad, but not your files in iCloud Drive.
