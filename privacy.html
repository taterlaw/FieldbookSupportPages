Summary

  • Fieldbook doesn't send any data to us. We run no servers and have no user accounts.
  • Your work orders are stored on your iPad and in the iCloud Drive of the Apple Account signed in on it. We can't see them.
  • Fieldbook has no analytics, advertising, tracking or other third-party code, and it doesn't ask for any permissions.
  • You and your business control your information and decide who can access it.

The rest of this policy explains the details.

Information we collect

We don't collect personal information through Fieldbook.

No servers or accounts

We run no servers and have no user accounts. Fieldbook never sends data to us, so we can't see, access, recover or delete anything you put in the app.

No tracking or advertising

  • Fieldbook contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting or other third-party code.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites. That's why Fieldbook doesn't show an App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • We don't sell or share personal information, as U.S. state privacy laws define "sell" and "share", and we don't act as a data broker.

No permissions

Fieldbook doesn't ask for any permissions: no camera, location, contacts, microphone or tracking. To add photos, Fieldbook uses Apple's photo picker, so the app receives only the photos you pick, never your whole photo library.

Checks on your iPad

Fieldbook checks whether your iPad has a network connection and whether iCloud is signed in, only so it can show you the sync status. These checks happen on the iPad, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Analytics from Apple

If you've chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymized crash and usage data, as described in Apple's privacy policy. We use it only to fix problems and improve the app.

Where your information is stored

On your iPad

Fieldbook keeps the following in the app's private storage on your iPad:

  • work orders, including job details, notes, materials, crew names and hours, and the name of the person submitting
  • resized copies of the photos you choose
  • downloaded templates
  • your Crew List

In iCloud Drive

Fieldbook uses iCloud Drive so the office and the crew can share work. These files are stored in the Fieldbook folder of whichever Apple Account is signed in on the iPad:

  • the office's templates
  • when someone taps Submit, a work order file (the whole work order, including its photos) and a PDF report

Apple stores and syncs these files under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

Anyone with access to that Apple Account or that folder can see these files. Your business controls, and is responsible for, who has that access.

Services you choose to use

Some features pass information to another service, but only when you use them. Each service is governed by its provider's terms, not by this policy.

  • Open in Maps sends the job address to Apple Maps.
  • Email opens Apple's Mail composer. The email is sent through your own mail account, to the recipients you choose.
  • Share sends the PDF report wherever you choose.

Information about other people

Work orders can contain information about other people, such as customers' names, phone numbers and addresses, crew names and hours, and photos of job sites.

Your business decides what is entered in Fieldbook. It's responsible for:

  • having the right to collect that information
  • telling people about it where the law requires
  • following the laws that apply to it

We don't process this information on your business's behalf.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays until someone deletes it.

  • Deleting a work order in the app removes it from the iPad. Files already submitted to iCloud Drive stay there.
  • You can remove templates and Crew List names in the app.
  • To delete files in iCloud Drive, use the Files app or iCloud.com.
  • Deleting the Fieldbook app removes its data from the iPad, but not files in iCloud Drive.

Because we don't have your data, we can't delete or recover it for you.

Security

Your data is protected by the security built into iPadOS and iCloud, not by anything we run. Please use a passcode on your iPad and keep your Apple Account secure.

No way of storing or sending data is completely secure, so we can't guarantee the security of your information.

Children

Fieldbook isn't directed to children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age in your country. We don't knowingly collect children's information.

Your privacy rights

Privacy laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA) give people rights over their personal information, such as the right to access, correct, export or delete it. Similar laws apply in other places.

Because we don't collect or hold personal information through Fieldbook, we have nothing to access, correct, export or delete. You can do these things yourself:

  • On the iPad: open your work orders in Fieldbook, edit drafts, and delete work orders, templates and Crew List names. You can print, share or email a work order's PDF report.
  • In iCloud Drive: open, copy, move or delete the files in the Fieldbook folder with the Files app or on iCloud.com.
  • Through the business: if a business has entered information about you in Fieldbook, for example as its customer or crew member, contact that business. It controls its iPads, its Apple Account and its Fieldbook folder.
  • Through Apple: for information Apple holds in iCloud, see Apple's privacy policy.

International users
